FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions & Answers

Will servicing my car at Vantage void my factory warranty?

No. Under statutory consumer protection laws, independent servicing does not void your factory warranty provided manufacturer-specified components and procedures are followed, which we document in full.

Do you offer courtesy vehicles while my car is serviced?

Yes. We maintain a fleet of complimentary executive loaner vehicles for services requiring more than 4 hours, subject to reservation availability.

Are the spare parts used genuinely authentic?

100%. We source components directly from original equipment manufacturers and tier-1 suppliers, accompanied by manufacturer serial numbers and warranties.

How long does a computerized diagnostic scan take?

A full topology scan and live sensor telemetry evaluation takes approximately 45 minutes, followed by a detailed digital report explaining our findings.
